Macronutrients

Protein and carbohydrates provide about 4 kcal per gram. Fat provides about 9 kcal per gram. Alcohol provides about 7 kcal per gram, but this demo knowledge base does not focus on alcoholic drinks.

Protein is important for satiety and muscle maintenance. Carbohydrates are useful for training energy and meal satisfaction. Fat supports flavor and energy density, but small portions can add many calories.
