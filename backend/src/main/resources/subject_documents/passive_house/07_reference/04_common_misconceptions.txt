TITLE: Common Misconceptions and Corrections
TAGS: misconceptions, faq, troubleshooting

MISCONCEPTION: Passive House means no heating system.
CORRECTION: Loads are very low, but most climates still require controlled heating and sometimes cooling.

MISCONCEPTION: Airtight buildings cannot breathe and are unhealthy.
CORRECTION: Walls do not provide reliable fresh air. Airtightness controls unintended leakage; designed ventilation provides air quality.

MISCONCEPTION: Occupants must never open windows.
CORRECTION: Windows may be opened. Mechanical ventilation provides a reliable baseline and heat recovery when windows are closed.

MISCONCEPTION: More south-facing glass is always better.
CORRECTION: Solar gain can reduce winter heating but increase overheating, glare and cooling. Optimise by climate and orientation.

MISCONCEPTION: Triple glazing alone makes a building Passive House.
CORRECTION: Whole-building performance depends on envelope, bridges, airtightness, ventilation, services, geometry and quality assurance.

MISCONCEPTION: Mechanical ventilation is the same as air conditioning.
CORRECTION: Ventilation provides fresh air. Heating, cooling and humidity control are separate functions, though equipment may be integrated.

MISCONCEPTION: Thermal mass always saves energy.
CORRECTION: Mass helps when temperatures cycle and the building can discharge heat. In persistently hot conditions it may retain unwanted heat.

MISCONCEPTION: Natural materials are automatically sustainable.
CORRECTION: Durability, sourcing, transport, fire, moisture, health and end-of-life all matter.

MISCONCEPTION: Net-zero energy means zero carbon.
CORRECTION: Annual energy balance, operational emissions and whole-life carbon are different accounting concepts.

MISCONCEPTION: A final blower-door test is enough.
CORRECTION: Intermediate testing finds defects while they remain repairable.

MISCONCEPTION: Certification criteria are universal building-code requirements.
CORRECTION: Certification is voluntary in many jurisdictions and does not replace local code compliance.
