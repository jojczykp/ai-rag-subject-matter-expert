TITLE: Sources and Further Reading
TAGS: sources, standards, official, references

Primary organisations and standards to verify for current project requirements:

Passive House Institute — official principles, certification criteria, component certification, PHPP and research:
https://passivehouse.com/

Passipedia — Passive House technical knowledge resource:
https://passipedia.org/

International Passive House Association:
https://passivehouse-international.org/

ISO 52000 series — framework and calculation methods for energy performance of buildings:
https://www.iso.org/standard/65601.html
https://www.iso.org/standard/65696.html

United States Department of Energy — building energy efficiency, zero-energy and passive design resources:
https://www.energy.gov/cmei/buildings

European Commission — energy performance of buildings and EU policy context:
https://energy.ec.europa.eu/topics/energy-efficiency/energy-efficient-buildings_en

Local requirements remain authoritative for building code, fire safety, structure, accessibility, ventilation, energy, moisture, radon and environmental regulation.

SOURCE NOTE
The corpus is an original educational synthesis. It paraphrases widely accepted building-science principles and uses official organisations as reference points. It does not reproduce proprietary standards or certification manuals. Criteria can change; confirm the current edition before relying on a threshold.
