TITLE: Design and Construction Checklists
TAGS: checklist, design-review, construction, commissioning

CONCEPT DESIGN
- Define conditioned envelope and treated floor area.
- Record climate, occupancy, setpoints and internal gains.
- Set energy, airtightness, overheating, carbon and water targets.
- Test form factor, orientation, glazing and shading.
- Reserve space for ducts, equipment, insulation and maintenance.

ENVELOPE DESIGN
- Trace continuous thermal and airtight lines.
- Calculate significant thermal bridges.
- Define window position, support, insulation and seals.
- Coordinate rain control, vapour control and drying paths.
- Minimise penetrations and design service entries.
- Review fire, acoustic, structural and radon interfaces.

SERVICES DESIGN
- Calculate project-specific heating and cooling loads.
- Size ventilation ducts for low pressure and noise.
- Locate intake and exhaust away from pollution sources.
- Define filtration, condensate and frost protection.
- Minimise domestic hot-water distribution losses.
- Define control sequence and metering.

PROCUREMENT
- Require verified product performance and installation data.
- Review substitutions across all disciplines.
- Obtain samples of membranes, tapes and penetrations.
- Confirm maintenance access and replacement parts.

CONSTRUCTION
- Run envelope workshop and build mock-ups.
- Inspect substrates before sealing.
- Photograph concealed layers.
- Protect completed membranes and insulation.
- Test airtightness before finishes conceal defects.
- Seal and test ducts; keep them clean.

COMMISSIONING
- Balance all ventilation terminals.
- Verify fan modes, bypass, frost protection and alarms.
- Test heating, cooling, hot water and controls.
- Record settings and measured performance.
- Train users and provide a concise guide.
- Schedule seasonal follow-up.
