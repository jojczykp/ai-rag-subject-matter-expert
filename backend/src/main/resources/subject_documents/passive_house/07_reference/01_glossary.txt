TITLE: Glossary
TAGS: glossary, definitions, terminology

Airtight layer: The continuous material or system that limits uncontrolled air leakage through the envelope.
Blower-door test: A fan-based test that measures building leakage at a pressure difference, commonly 50 Pa.
Conditioned space: Space intentionally maintained within defined temperature or humidity conditions.
Embodied carbon: Greenhouse-gas emissions associated with materials and construction over specified life-cycle stages.
ERV: Energy-recovery ventilator; transfers sensible heat and some moisture between air streams.
Form factor: A measure relating envelope area to treated floor area or volume; definitions vary.
g-value: Fraction of incident solar energy transmitted through glazing, including secondary heat transfer; broadly similar to SHGC but definitions can differ.
Heat bridge / thermal bridge: Local region of elevated heat flow due to geometry, material or insulation discontinuity.
Heat-recovery efficiency: Fraction of extract-air heat transferred to supply air under stated conditions.
HRV: Heat-recovery ventilator; primarily transfers sensible heat.
Hygrothermal: Relating to coupled heat and moisture behaviour.
MVHR: Mechanical ventilation with heat recovery, usually balanced supply and extract.
n50: Air changes per hour at a 50 Pa test pressure, based on test volume.
Operative temperature: Comfort-related combination of air temperature and mean radiant temperature.
Passive House: A performance-based building standard and design approach focused on very low energy demand and high comfort.
Passive solar design: Use of building form, orientation, glazing, shading and mass to respond to solar energy without active collection equipment.
PHPP: Passive House Planning Package, a calculation and design tool used for Passive House projects.
Primary energy: Energy measure that accounts for upstream conversion and delivery according to defined factors.
Psi-value: Linear thermal transmittance of a junction in W/(m·K).
q50: Air leakage at 50 Pa divided by envelope area.
R-value: Thermal resistance. Higher values indicate greater resistance to heat flow.
SHGC: Solar heat-gain coefficient, the fraction of incident solar energy entering through a window.
Treated floor area: Project floor-area convention used in Passive House calculations; it is not necessarily gross floor area.
U-value: Thermal transmittance in W/(m²·K). Lower values indicate less heat flow.
Vapour retarder: Layer that limits vapour diffusion to a defined degree; not necessarily a complete vapour barrier.
Windtight layer: Exterior-side layer that prevents wind washing through insulation while allowing suitable drying.
Whole-life carbon: Carbon impacts across construction, operation, maintenance, replacement and end-of-life within a stated boundary.
