Low-calorie meal design

Low-calorie dishes work best when they combine lean protein, high-volume vegetables, and strong low-calorie flavors. Useful flavor builders include lemon, vinegar, garlic, mustard, herbs, spices, chili, and tomato.

Limit energy-dense additions: oils, butter, cheese, nuts, nut butter, avocado, chocolate, and honey. Use grams for these ingredients.
