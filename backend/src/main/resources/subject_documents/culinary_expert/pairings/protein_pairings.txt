Protein pairing guide

Chicken pairs with rice, potatoes, broccoli, mushrooms, lemon, garlic, and yogurt sauces. Salmon pairs with lemon, dill, rice, asparagus, broccoli, and soy-ginger flavors. Tofu pairs with soy sauce, ginger, garlic, broccoli, rice, peanut, and chili. Lentils pair with tomato, carrot, onion, spinach, cumin, and lemon.
