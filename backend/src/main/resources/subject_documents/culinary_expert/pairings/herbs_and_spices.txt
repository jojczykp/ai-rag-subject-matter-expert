Herbs and spices pairing guide

Chicken: garlic, lemon, paprika, rosemary, thyme, parsley.
Fish: lemon, dill, parsley, garlic, black pepper.
Beans and lentils: cumin, paprika, chili, garlic, tomato, onion.
Eggs: chives, black pepper, spinach, mushrooms, tomato.
Yogurt sauces: garlic, cucumber, dill, lemon, mint.
