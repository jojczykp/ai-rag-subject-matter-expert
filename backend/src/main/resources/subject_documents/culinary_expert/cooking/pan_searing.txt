Pan searing

Pan searing uses high surface heat to brown food quickly. It is useful for chicken, fish, tofu, beef, mushrooms, and vegetables. Dry the surface before searing because excess water delays browning. Add oil after the pan is hot, then place food in a single layer.

For nutrition calculations, include the oil that remains in the dish. If the amount of absorbed oil is unknown, count the full amount added unless the recipe explicitly discards it.
