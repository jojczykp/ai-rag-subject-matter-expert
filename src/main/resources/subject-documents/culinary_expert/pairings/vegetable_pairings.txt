Vegetable pairing guide

Broccoli pairs well with garlic, lemon, chicken, tofu, rice, and sesame. Spinach pairs with eggs, lentils, chickpeas, garlic, feta-like salty cheese, and yogurt. Tomato pairs with basil, garlic, olive oil, beans, eggs, tuna, and pasta. Mushrooms pair with eggs, beef, garlic, thyme, onion, and butter.
