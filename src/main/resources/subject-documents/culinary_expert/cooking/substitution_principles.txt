Substitution principles

Substitute by function first, then by nutrition. A protein ingredient should usually be replaced with another protein ingredient. A fat source should be replaced with another fat source. A starchy base can be replaced with another grain, potato, sweet potato, or legume.

For lower calories, reduce oils, butter, cheese, nuts, and creamy sauces first. For higher protein, increase lean meat, fish, tofu, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, or legumes.
