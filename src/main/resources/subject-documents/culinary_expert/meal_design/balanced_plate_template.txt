Balanced plate template

A practical balanced plate uses four components: protein, vegetables, carbohydrate base, and fat or sauce. Example proportions: 25-35% protein, 35-50% vegetables, 20-35% carbohydrate, and a small measured fat source.

This template is flexible. For muscle gain, increase protein and total calories. For weight loss, keep protein high and reduce energy-dense fats and refined carbohydrates. For endurance, increase carbohydrate base.
