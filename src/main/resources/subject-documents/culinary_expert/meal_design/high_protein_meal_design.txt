High-protein meal design

A high-protein main meal typically contains 30-50 g protein. Start with one primary protein: chicken breast, turkey breast, lean beef, salmon, cod, tuna, tofu, tempeh, eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, lentils, beans, or chickpeas.

Then add vegetables for volume and micronutrients, a carbohydrate base if the user wants energy, and a measured fat source for flavor. If calories are limited, choose lean proteins and vegetables, and keep oils, nuts, cheese, and sauces small.
