Vegetarian meal design

Vegetarian meals can use eggs, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, cheese, tofu, tempeh, lentils, beans, chickpeas, quinoa, nuts, and seeds. To improve protein, combine a dense protein source such as tofu, tempeh, Greek yogurt, cottage cheese, eggs, or legumes with whole grains and vegetables.

For vegan meals, avoid eggs and dairy and rely on tofu, tempeh, legumes, grains, nuts, and seeds.
